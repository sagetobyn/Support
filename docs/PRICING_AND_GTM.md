# Pricing And GTM

SupportWaala is positioned as a business-improvement product for Indian e-commerce sellers. RTOShield by SupportWaala is the first service-product: RTO/NDR profit recovery.

RTO/NDR is the first wedge because it is painful, measurable, and action-oriented. The long-term product is a profit-maximizing e-commerce operations control room.

## Target Customer

Indian D2C sellers doing 500 to 5,000 orders per month, with high COD share and meaningful RTO pain.

First niche:

- Fashion.
- Footwear.

Secondary niches:

- Beauty.
- Accessories.

## Offer

Free profit leakage audit plus 14-day RTO/NDR profit recovery pilot.

Audit promise:

> Share your last 30 days order/shipment/NDR CSV. We will show where profit leakage is coming from, which pincodes/couriers/SKUs are leaking money, which COD orders should be converted to prepaid, and what can be saved.

Pilot promise:

> For 14 days, we help your ops team work a daily profit action queue: confirm risky COD orders, correct weak addresses, push prepaid offers, rescue NDR cases, review courier/pincode leakage, and report estimated savings.

## Objections

### "Will customers get irritated?"

Response:

We do not message every customer. We focus on risky COD orders and failed delivery cases. Low-risk and prepaid orders can move normally.

### "We already use Shiprocket/NimbusPost."

Response:

That is fine. We work on top of your current shipping stack. The issue is not only data visibility. It is acting quickly and proving which actions saved money.

### "We already use a COD confirmation app."

Response:

That solves only pre-dispatch confirmation. We also handle address quality, NDR rescue, daily ops actions, and savings proof.

### "Who updates the courier dashboard?"

Response:

In the MVP, your ops team or managed pilot operator updates it manually from the action queue. Later integrations can automate the handoff.

### "How do we know it worked?"

Response:

We start with a baseline and show weekly numbers: orders held, cancellations before shipping, addresses corrected, NDRs rescued, RTO avoided, and estimated INR saved.
